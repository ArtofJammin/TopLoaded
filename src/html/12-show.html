The show spans three spaces: Triple Crown Ballroom, pre-function room, and business center. The current table plan covers the ballroom only; layouts for the other two spaces will be added when available.

Find Top Loaded in the business center: four tables along the wall by the entrance door, plus a fifth table forming an L for trades. These are the shop's booth, not separate vendor openings.

Ballroom access: three entrances on the pre-function side—near, middle, and far relative to the entrance. Keep all three access paths clear.
